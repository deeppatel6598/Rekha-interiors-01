01 · Colour

Twelve surfaces, one accent

Photography carries the colour on this site, so the interface stays neutral. Each swatch below lists the contrast ratio it was chosen to meet — every text pair clears WCAG AA at 4.5:1, and every UI pair clears 3:1.

02 · Typography

Newsreader for the voice, Manrope for the detail

A serif display face against a geometric sans keeps headlines warm and body copy plain. Every step is a fluid clamp, so nothing needs re-tuning per breakpoint and no size collides with the one above it.

04 · Motion

Six entrances, one rhythm

Scroll this section up and down — each tile replays from the edge it left by. Entrances ease out over 620ms, exits ease in over 160ms, and grid children stagger 60ms apart. Everything animates transform and opacity only, and the whole layer switches off under prefers-reduced-motion.
