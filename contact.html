Common questions

Before you write in

What should I send with the first message?
The carpet area, when you get possession, and a sentence about how you use the rooms. If you have the builder's floor plan, attach it — it saves a visit.
How quickly will you reply?
Within two working days. If we think the budget and the brief do not meet, we will say so in that first reply rather than after a site visit.
Is the first consultation charged?
No. The first meeting — on site in Mumbai or Thane, or over video anywhere else — is free, and so is the initial scope and budget range. Fees begin once you ask for drawings.
Which areas do you cover on site?
Mumbai and Thane, including Vihang Hills, Hiranandani, the Lodha developments at Palava and Parel, and the surrounding suburbs. Everywhere else is covered by virtual design.
Can I just get styling for a flat that is already done?
Yes. Visual styling runs as a standalone project — furniture layout, lighting, textiles, art and accessories, with no demolition and no contractor.
