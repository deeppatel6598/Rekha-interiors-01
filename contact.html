Common questions

Before you write in

What should I send with the first message?
The carpet area, the possession date and a sentence about how you use the rooms. If you have the builder's floor plan, attach it — it saves a visit.
How quickly will you reply?
Within two working days, with a scope and an honest price band. If we think the budget and the brief do not meet, we will say so in that first reply rather than after a site visit.
Is the first consultation charged?
No. The first meeting, the site measure and the initial scope and price band are free. Detailed drawings begin once the scope is agreed.
Which areas do you work in?
Pune and the immediate surroundings — Baner, Balewadi, Hinjewadi, Kharadi, NIBM Road, Wakad and the riverfront developments among them.
