Samiksha is an interior designer and visual stylist, and a digital creator with a hundred thousand people following the process on Instagram — detail drawings, kitchen breakdowns, mirror units, compact TV walls, the lot.

How a project runs

Four stages, agreed up front

01
First consultation

We walk the flat, or the video call, and talk about how you live in it. Free of charge.

02
Layout & 3D

Room-by-room layouts, joinery details and a 3D view, so you can judge it before it is made.

03
Execution support

Drawings for the contractor, material selections, and site visits while the work runs.

04
Styling & handover

The last ten per cent — lighting, textiles, art and the shelf that finally looks right.
