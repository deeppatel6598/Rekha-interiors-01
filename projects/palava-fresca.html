Palava Fresca, Dombivli

A first home on a fixed budget, where the decision was to finish three rooms properly rather than half-finish five.

Palava Fresca, Dombivli
All projects The work Photographs Book a consult
The brief

What the home needed

The owners had a firm number and no interest in pretending otherwise. We put the whole budget into the pieces that are expensive to change later — the kitchen, the wardrobes and the electrical layout — and left the decorative layer for them to add over the following year.

That meant honest material choices. Laminate rather than veneer on the wardrobe shutters, a quartz counter only on the wet run and a good laminate elsewhere, and surface lighting instead of a full false ceiling.

Nothing here is temporary, though. The layout leaves clean junction points for the pieces they plan to add, so the next stage can happen without undoing any of the first.
