La Vida, Baner

An open plan living and dining room for a family who cook every evening and wanted the kitchen to stay part of the room rather than hide behind a wall.

La Vida, Baner
All projects The work Photographs Free quote
The brief

What the flat needed

The flat came to us with a dividing wall between the kitchen and the dining area, which left the dining table in permanent shade. We took the wall out, moved the plumbing stack and ran a single counter from the hob through to a breakfast ledge, so one person can cook while the rest of the family sits at the table.

Storage does the structural work here. A full-height unit along the corridor holds everything that used to sit on the floor, and the TV wall is a single sheet of oak veneer with the media unit recessed into it, so the living room reads as one clean plane from the entrance.

Finishes stay warm and quiet: matt laminate on the joinery, a honed stone counter that will not show water marks, and warm-white lighting on three separate circuits so the room can be bright for homework and low for guests.
