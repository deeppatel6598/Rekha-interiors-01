Eon Homes, Hinjewadi

A compact two-bedroom flat for a couple who work from home, where the kitchen counter had to double as a desk on weekday mornings.

Eon Homes, Hinjewadi
All projects The work Photographs Free quote
The brief

What the flat needed

The brief was short: a kitchen that works for two cooks, a place to sit with a laptop that is not the dining table, and no wasted centimetres anywhere. At 1,050 square feet, every decision had to earn its floor area.

We built the kitchen along one wall in an L, with the tall units carrying the oven and the pantry so the working counter stays clear. The return leg extends past the hob into a 900mm ledge with power below it — that is the desk, and it disappears again when the laptop closes.

The bedrooms took sliding wardrobes rather than hinged doors, which returns close to half a metre of circulation to each room. A mirror on one leaf of the master wardrobe meant the room never needed a separate dressing unit.
