Hiranandani Estate, Thane

A finished flat that had never felt finished. No demolition and no contractor — a styling project that re-planned the furniture, the lighting and everything on the shelves.

Hiranandani Estate, Thane

The brief

What the home needed

The owners had done the hard work already: good joinery, sound finishes, nothing that needed pulling out. The flat still felt like a showroom nobody had moved into — furniture pushed to the walls, one flat ceiling light per room, empty surfaces.

Almost all of it came down to layout and light. Pulling the sofa off the wall and floating it against a rug made the living room read as a room rather than a corridor, and three lamp circuits at different heights replaced the single overhead.

The rest is the layer people underestimate: textiles with some weight to them, art hung at the right height, plants that suit the light each room actually gets, and open shelves styled in groups instead of spread evenly.
