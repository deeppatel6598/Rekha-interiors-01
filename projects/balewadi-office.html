Balewadi office

A thirty-desk office delivered from a single shell-and-core handover, with the meeting rooms acoustically separated from the open floor.

Balewadi office

The brief

What the flat needed

A commercial fit-out is a scheduling problem before it is a design problem. The client had a fixed move-in date tied to their lease, so the drawing set was frozen early and the joinery was made in our workshop while the services were still being run on site.

The floor plate is arranged with the open desks along the window line and the enclosed rooms pulled into the core, which keeps daylight on the people who sit down all day. Two meeting rooms are built with double-glazed partitions and insulated ceilings, so a call in one is not audible at the desks outside it.

Everything the client will need to change later is modular. The workstations sit on a standard grid, the power comes up through floor boxes on that same grid, and the storage runs are demountable — adding ten desks next year is a weekend, not another fit-out.
