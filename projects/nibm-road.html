NIBM Road

A family home built around three bedrooms that each had to serve a different age, from a nursery through to a room a teenager would not outgrow in two years.

NIBM Road
All projects The work Photographs Free quote
The brief

What the flat needed

Three bedrooms, three completely different briefs. The master needed quiet and a large wardrobe; the middle room needed a study that a nine-year-old could still use at fifteen; the smallest had to work as a nursery now and a guest room later.

Rather than three unrelated schemes we set one language — oak veneer, off-white laminate, a single brass detail on the handles — and changed only the proportions between rooms. The result reads as one home instead of three showrooms.

The lighting plan carries most of the atmosphere. Cove lighting around the perimeter of each ceiling gives an even wash for the daytime, and separately switched bedside and desk circuits mean nobody has to light the whole room at eleven at night.
