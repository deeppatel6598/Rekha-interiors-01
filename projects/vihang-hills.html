Vihang Hills, Thane

A two-bedroom flat high in a Thane tower, planned around a living room that had to hold a dining table, a work corner and the view without feeling crowded.

Vihang Hills, Thane

The brief

What the home needed

The flat's best asset is height — a long window line and a lot of afternoon light. The builder's layout wasted it by putting a solid partition between the living and dining areas, which left the dining table in shade for most of the day.

Taking that partition out gave one room that reads the full length of the glass. Everything along the window wall is kept below sill height so nothing casts a shadow back into the room, and the tall storage is pushed to the opposite side.

The TV wall is a single plane of oak veneer with the media unit recessed into it, so the living room reads clean from the entrance. Finishes stay warm and quiet: matt laminate on the joinery and a honed counter that will not show water marks.
