Lodha Palava, Dombivli

A compact two-bedroom home for a couple who both work from home, where the kitchen counter had to double as a desk on weekday mornings.

Lodha Palava, Dombivli

The brief

What the home needed

The brief was short: a kitchen that works for two cooks, somewhere to sit with a laptop that is not the dining table, and no wasted centimetres anywhere. Under a thousand square feet, every decision had to earn its floor area.

The kitchen runs along one wall in an L, with the tall units carrying the oven and the pantry so the working counter stays clear. The return leg extends past the hob into a 900mm ledge with power below it — that is the desk, and it disappears again when the laptop closes.

Both bedrooms took sliding wardrobes rather than hinged doors, which returns close to half a metre of circulation to each room. A mirror on one leaf of the master wardrobe meant the room never needed a separate dressing unit.
