01 · Full home interiors

Every room planned together, so the flat reads as one home

A full home is the job this studio is set up for. We take the flat from possession to the day it is finished: layouts, joinery drawings, ceilings and lighting, material selections, and the styling at the end.

Planning every room at once is what keeps a home from looking like five separate showrooms. One material language, one lighting logic, and proportions that change room to room without the scheme falling apart.

  • Room-by-room layouts and 3D views
  • Dimensioned joinery drawings for your contractor
  • False ceiling, cove and task lighting plans
  • Material, finish and hardware selections
  • Final styling before you move in

02 · Modular kitchen

Built around how you actually cook

An Indian kitchen takes more heat, more water and more daily use than the catalogue photographs suggest. We plan the working triangle around your routine first, then choose materials that survive it.

Tall units carry the pantry and the appliances so the working counter stays clear, and the wet run gets a surface that will not stain. Every drawer is sized against what goes in it, not against a standard module list.

  • Working-triangle layout planned around your routine
  • Tall units, pull-outs and corner solutions
  • Counter and splashback selections
  • Chimney, hob and sink cut-outs coordinated
  • Hardware specified and scheduled

03 · Wardrobes & storage

The half-metre a sliding door gives back

Storage is where a flat is won or lost. We measure what you own before we draw the wardrobe — sari lengths, suitcase heights, how many shirts hang and how many fold.

In tight bedrooms a sliding shutter returns close to half a metre of circulation over a hinged door. In larger rooms, hinged doors give you the full opening. We will tell you which one your room wants, and why.

  • Sliding, hinged and walk-in layouts
  • Internal drawers, lofts and pull-down rails
  • Mirror, laminate, veneer or lacquer shutters
  • Loft storage across the full run
  • Study tables, dressers and TV units built in

04 · Visual styling

The last ten per cent, which is most of the feeling

A room can be built perfectly and still feel unfinished. Styling is the layer that fixes that: lighting temperature, textiles, art, plants, the objects on the open shelf and the things taken off it.

This runs as part of a full project, or on its own for a home that is already built. For an existing flat it is often the cheapest change that makes the biggest difference — no demolition, no contractor, a few weeks.

  • Furniture and layout re-planning
  • Lighting, textiles and soft furnishing selections
  • Art, mirrors and accessory sourcing
  • Shelf and surface styling
  • Shoot-ready finish for listing or handover

05 · Virtual design

The same drawings, wherever you live

If your home is outside Mumbai and Thane, virtual design covers the same ground over video. You send measurements and photographs, we work through the rooms on call, and you get the full drawing set.

What you receive is buildable by any competent local contractor: dimensioned layouts, 3D views, a material and finish list, and a shopping list with sizes. We stay on call through the build for the questions that come up.

  • Measured layouts and 3D views from your photographs
  • Material, finish and hardware list
  • Shopping list with dimensions and quantities
  • Scheduled video reviews through the project
  • Available anywhere in India

06 · Renovation & makeovers

Working in a home that is already lived in

Renovating an occupied flat is a different job from fitting out an empty one. The work is sequenced so a kitchen or a bathroom is never out of use for longer than agreed, and the disruption stays in the room being worked on.

Often the answer is smaller than people expect. A re-planned layout, new shutters on sound carcasses, better lighting and a styling pass can transform a flat for a fraction of a full strip-out.

  • Survey of what is worth keeping
  • Phased plan for an occupied home
  • Re-facing and re-planning existing joinery
  • Lighting and electrical improvements
  • Full styling pass at the end

How a project runs

Four stages, agreed up front

Every project runs on the same four stages, and you know what is coming at each one before you commit to anything.

01

First consultation

We walk the flat — or do it over video — and talk about how you live in it, what you own and what the budget is. Free of charge, and there is no obligation after it.

02

Layout & 3D

Room-by-room layouts first, then joinery details and a 3D view you can actually judge. Nothing goes to a contractor until the drawings are signed off.

03

Execution support

Your contractor gets a drawing set they can price and build from. We help compare quotes, approve samples, and visit site while the work runs.

04

Styling & handover

Lighting, textiles, art and accessories go in last, chosen with the scheme rather than bought afterwards. The room is finished on the day you move in.

Before you ask

Common questions

What does the design fee cover?
The drawing set and the decisions behind it — layouts, joinery details, elevations, lighting, and the material and finish selections. Furniture, joinery and site works are paid to your contractor and suppliers directly, so you always see what those actually cost.
Do you do the execution as well?
We are a design and styling studio, not a contracting firm. You get drawings any competent contractor can build from, we help you compare quotes, and we visit site while the work runs. If you would like an introduction to contractors we have worked with, we can make one.
How long does a full home take?
Design usually runs three to five weeks from the first meeting to a signed-off drawing set, depending on how quickly decisions come back. The build after that is your contractor's programme — typically eight to sixteen weeks for a full flat.
Do you work with a budget I set?
Yes, and we would rather hear the number at the first meeting. Knowing it lets us put the money into the things that are expensive to change later, and leave the decorative layer for you to add over time.
Can you help if the flat is already finished?
That is what visual styling is for. No demolition and no contractor — re-planned furniture layouts, better lighting, textiles, art and accessories. It is usually the cheapest change that makes the biggest difference.
I do not live in Mumbai. Can you still help?
Yes, through virtual design. You send measurements and photographs, we work through the rooms over video, and you get the same drawing set plus a shopping list your local contractor can work from.
