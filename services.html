01 · Full home interiors

Everything inside the shell, drawn and built by one team

A full home is the job we are set up for. We take the flat from developer handover to the day you move in: layouts, joinery, ceilings, electricals, painting and the finishes in between, on one contract and one schedule.

Because the drawings and the carpentry are ours, the built room matches the render. There is no second party to renegotiate with when a detail turns out to be awkward on site.

  • Space planning and 3D walkthrough
  • All joinery made in our workshop
  • False ceilings, cove and task lighting
  • Electrical and plumbing coordination
  • Painting, polishing and final finishes

02 · Modular kitchen

Built around how you actually cook

An Indian kitchen takes more heat, more water and more daily use than the catalogue photographs suggest. We plan the working triangle around your routine first, then choose materials that survive it.

Carcasses in marine-grade ply, branded soft-close hardware, and a counter chosen for the way you use the wet run rather than for the sample board.

  • Marine-grade ply carcass, sealed edges
  • Soft-close hinges and channels, warranted
  • Quartz, granite or solid-surface counters
  • Tall units, pull-outs and corner solutions
  • Chimney, hob and sink cut-outs coordinated

03 · Wardrobes & storage

The half-metre a sliding door gives back

Storage is where a small flat is won or lost. We measure what you own before we draw the wardrobe — sari lengths, suitcase heights, how many shirts hang and how many fold.

In tight bedrooms a sliding shutter returns close to half a metre of circulation over a hinged door. In larger rooms, hinged doors give you the full opening. We will tell you which one your room wants.

  • Sliding, hinged and walk-in layouts
  • Internal drawers, lofts and pull-down rails
  • Mirror, laminate, veneer or lacquer shutters
  • Loft storage over the full run
  • Study tables and dressers built in

04 · Renovation

Working in a home that is already lived in

Renovating an occupied flat is a different job from fitting out an empty one. The work is sequenced so that a bathroom or a kitchen is never out of use for longer than agreed, and the dust is contained to the room being worked on.

We survey the existing services before quoting, so the price accounts for what is behind the wall rather than discovering it halfway through.

  • Pre-work survey of existing services
  • Phased schedule for occupied homes
  • Dust containment and daily clean-up
  • Structural and waterproofing checks
  • Re-use of sound existing joinery where sensible

06 · Commercial & office

Fit-outs that hit the lease date

A commercial fit-out is a scheduling problem before it is a design problem. Drawings are frozen early, joinery is made in the workshop while services are being run on site, and the programme is built backwards from your move-in date.

We plan for what changes later: workstations on a standard grid, power on the same grid, and demountable storage runs so adding desks next year is a weekend, not a refit.

  • Open-plan desking on a standard grid
  • Acoustically separated meeting rooms
  • Reception, pantry and breakout areas
  • Floor boxes and cable management
  • Programme built to the lease date

How a project runs

Four stages, dated in advance

Every job runs on the same four stages, and every stage carries a date from the day the drawings are signed off.

01

Site measure

We measure the flat ourselves rather than working from the builder's plan, and note how the light moves through it during the day. Existing services are surveyed at the same visit.

02

Drawing & 3D

Layouts first, then joinery details, then a walkthrough you can actually judge. Nothing is cut until the drawings and the itemised quote are both signed off.

03

Execution

Joinery is made in our workshop while site works run in parallel. One foreman runs the trades, and you get a progress update every week with photographs.

04

Handover

You walk the flat and write the snag list. We close it, hand over the warranty papers and the hardware manuals, and clean the flat before you move in.

Before you ask

Common questions

How is the price worked out?
Item by item. The quote lists every piece of joinery with its size, its core material and its finish, plus the site works and the finishes. Nothing is quoted per square foot of carpet area, because that hides which room the money is going into.
How long does a full home take?
A two-bedroom flat is usually nine to twelve weeks from the day the drawings are signed off; three and four-bedroom homes run fourteen to twenty. You get the dated stage plan before work starts, and we tell you as soon as a date is at risk.
Do you work with a budget I set?
Yes, and we would rather hear the number at the first meeting. Knowing the budget lets us put it into the things that are expensive to redo later — kitchen, wardrobes, electricals — and leave the decorative work for you to add over time.
What warranty do I get?
Ten years on the modular carcasses and one year on site works and finishes, with the hardware carrying its own manufacturer warranty. All of it is handed over in writing at the end of the job.
Can you work while we are living in the flat?
For renovations, yes. The programme is phased so a kitchen or bathroom is never out of use for longer than we have agreed, with dust screens and a clean-up at the end of every day.
Do you take on single rooms?
We take on single kitchens, single wardrobes and one-room refits. What we do not do is supply drawings for someone else to build — the value of this studio is that the same team does both.
