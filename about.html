The studio

One contract, from the first sketch to the last snag

Rekha Interiors & Developers is a design and execution studio working out of Pune. Most studios draw a scheme and then hand it to a contractor. We do both, which removes the gap where budgets slip and details get quietly dropped.

That means one set of drawings, one schedule and one person answering the phone. Our carpenters, electricians and painters are our own, and the joinery is made in our workshop rather than improvised on site.

How we work

Four things we will not trade away

01

One price, written down

The quote lists every item with its material and its finish. If something is not on it, it is not in the price — and we say so before you sign, not after.

02

Dates that hold

Every stage carries a date at the start of the job. When a date is at risk you hear it from us first, with the reason and the new date.

03

Materials you can check

Marine-grade ply on the carcasses, branded hardware, and the invoices to prove it. Samples are approved on site before anything is ordered.

04

We finish the snag list

Handover is not the day the work stops. It is the day the last item on your list is closed and the warranty papers are in your hand.

Who you will deal with

A small team, on purpose

The person who measures your flat is the person who draws it, and they stay on the job until handover. There is no account manager in between, and no point in the project where you have to explain the brief a second time.

On site, the same foreman runs the trades from first day to last. Design decisions get made in front of the wall in question rather than over email.
